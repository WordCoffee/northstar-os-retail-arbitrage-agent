FAQ

Quick answers

Is retail arbitrage sourcing from Costco receipts safe for scaling on Amazon?

Not by default. Costco retail receipts alone are often insufficient for invoice legitimacy at scale — Northstar flags this risk on every screened product rather than assuming it away.

Does Northstar replace Keepa or SellerAmp?

Northstar focuses on portfolio-level sourcing, profit modeling, and DFW logistics together — you can still use price-history tools alongside it for deeper historical charts.

What counts as a "qualified" deal?

A product that clears your net profit floor, ROI target, seller-count range, and logistics risk threshold simultaneously — not just one metric in isolation.
