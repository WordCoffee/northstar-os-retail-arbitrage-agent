# Ad creative

Read `product-marketing` first. Facts and claim rules come from there; this skill only shapes
the copy.

## Headlines

- 2–5 options, each under 60 characters, each making **one** claim.
- The first three words must survive truncation: lead with the outcome, not the brand.
- No exclamation marks, no fake urgency, no "shop now" inside a headline.

## Body / primary text

- 90–150 characters for Sponsored Products; longer only when the placement allows it.
- One benefit, one proof, one action. If you have three benefits, write three variants.
- Speak to the search intent that triggered the ad, not to the brand's whole story.

## CTA

- Match the funnel stage. A discovery placement gets "See details", a retargeting placement gets
  "Add to cart". Never promise a discount that is not in the supplied facts.

## Compliance

- Never state or imply the ad is live, approved, uploaded or spending.
- No competitor trademarks, no "Amazon's choice", no review counts or star ratings unless
  supplied as verified facts.
- Every claim in the ad must be defensible from the product facts. Flag, do not soften.

## Variant discipline

When asked for variants, vary the **angle** (price, convenience, durability, occasion), not just
the adjectives. Three reworded copies of one angle is one ad.
