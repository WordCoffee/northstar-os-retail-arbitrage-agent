In today's video, I'm going to show you the best mockup tools you can use as a UI designer to create
beautiful presentations and portfolio shots. Let's begin. The first tool on our list is
Brandbird. Brandbird is an online tool that allows you to create realistic product mockups
without needing any design experience. To use Brandbird, you can pick a template,
upload your design and customize it easily. You can modify the background,
the perspective of the device and much more. Once you are happy with your design, you can download
your mockup as a high resolution image. The next tool is called Artboard. It's a web based tool
that allows you to design and create animated mockups in one place. It has lots of high quality
templates for a variety of purposes. And its user interface is very well designed. You can
create your mockup from scratch or just select a template and start editing it. Once you are
done, you can either animate it or export it as an image. Make sure to give it a try. The next tool
on our list is actually a Figma plugin called Mockup. This plugin allows you to easily create
realistic mockups of your designs directly within Figma. It provides you with a big
collection of high quality mockups and it's so easy to work with. You can select the mockup
template you want to use such as a phone, tablet or laptop. Then choose a frame and your design
will be added to the device instantly. You can even customize the color of these devices. It's
awesome. Next we have Clean Mock. Just like other mockup tools, it has many different
templates you can choose from. From desktop devices to iOS and Android devices. It even
has some dribble templates that you can use for your dribble shots. The next tool is Rotato.
Rotato is a tool designed for creating realistic 3D product mockups and animations for marketing
and advertising purposes. It allows you to easily import 2D designs and turn them into 3D product
mockups. With Rotato you can add realistic lighting and shadows, adjust camera angles
and create animations that showcase your designs in action. It allows you to export your
mockup as an image or a high quality animated video. The next tool on our list is Glorify. Glorify is an
online design tool that allows you to create professional looking product mockups for e-commerce
websites and social media. It's quite similar to Canva and it has a large library of templates
for social media posts, ads, device mockups etc. It has an easy to use interface allowing
you to customize everything with ease. If you want to learn how to design UI cards like a pro,
