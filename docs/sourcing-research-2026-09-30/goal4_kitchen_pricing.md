## E. Risk & packaging notes

- **Fragility:** glassware/ceramic → FBA damage/return cost; require stronger packaging or exclude.
- **Heavy/bulky:** cookware sets → dimensional-weight fees; excludes unless margin thick.
- **Brand control:** KitchenAid/Farberware/Libbey/OXO are channel-controlled → Amazon authorization likely required; do not assume.
- **Pack identity:** multi-pack glass sets swing unit price massively; confirm exact UPC + pack.
- **Electrical:** appliances need UL, warranty, return handling; higher risk.
- **Club competition:** basic drinkware/utensils are commonly club-won → low wholesale advantage expected.
