## Key cross-cutting truth (must not be violated)

1. Selling on Amazon requires **brand/rights permission** for many brands; a distributor invoice is **not** proof of brand authorization.
2. Marketplace/gig **invoices accepted by Amazon** require manufacturer/distributor invoices with quantity and (usually) matching business identity — many "wholesale" sites explicitly cannot guarantee brand resale permission.
3. **Faire is discovery-only** (prior project finding: Faire cannot fulfil Amazon orders and Faire goods cannot be listed on third-party marketplaces). Faire prices = `DISCOVERY_BENCHMARK_ONLY`.
4. No supplier in this program was contacted or applied to. Every account application is an owner action.
