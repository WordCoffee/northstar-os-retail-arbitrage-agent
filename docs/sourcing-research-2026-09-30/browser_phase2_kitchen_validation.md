## Exclusions applied (per brief)
- Food/beverage/supplement/cosmetics excluded (Down To Earth sells tea/coffee — exclude those lines).
- Fragile/heavy/bulky (glassware, large cookware sets) excluded unless exceptional economics.
- Amazon-native brands (Cook N Home) excluded (direct competition).
- Brands with likely brand-direct/1P competition and licensing (Melrose "licensed brands", Lifetime brands) → authorization risk.
