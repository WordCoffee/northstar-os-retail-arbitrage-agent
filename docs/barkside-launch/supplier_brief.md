## 4. Ingredient specifications

| **Turmeric** | Curcuminoids ≥ 3.0% · lead ≤ 1 ppm · aflatoxin B1 ≤ 5 ppb, total ≤ 10 ppb · moisture ≤ 8% · no added colour |

| **Rice bran** | Food grade, stabilised, ≤ 8% moisture |

Any carrier, anti-caking agent, flowing agent or added flavour in **any** input must be
declared on the COA. We are building a label on "no fillers" and cannot discover one
after print.

## 8. Questions the quote must answer

1. Price per kg at 40 / 100 / 250 kg per SKU, and price per filled 80 g pouch.
2. Minimum order per SKU, and minimum per ingredient.
3. Lead time: sample, first production, reorder.
4. Can you blend, fill, code and carton in-house, or is any step subcontracted?
5. **Organic option:** which of these inputs can you supply certified organic, and what
   is the price delta? (Turmeric and black pepper matter most.)
6. Is your blending/packing operation certified as an organic handler? Certificate number.
7. Allergen control: is mustard and celery run on the same line, and what is the
   changeover and cleaning validation?
8. Is any input irradiated or ETO-treated? (We require neither.)
9. Country of origin per input.
10. Payment terms and currency.
11. Can you hold finished stock and ship to a US 3PL or direct to Amazon FBA?

## 9. Evaluation rule

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Formula confidentiality | NDA signed, ownership written |

| Allergen control | Mustard/celery changeover documented — this is a real recall risk, not a formality |
| Commercial | MOQ reachable at 40 kg/SKU, lead time under 8 weeks |
| Documentation | Lot COA available **before** shipment, not after |

A supplier who cannot answer §8.5 and §8.6 is not disqualified — but they are a
conventional-only supplier, and the organic tier has to be sourced elsewhere.
