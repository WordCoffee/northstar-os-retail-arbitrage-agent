## 4. Private-label and packing capability — public investigation

Investigated directly on `vietnampepper.vn` on 2026-10-02.

**Observed (verbatim where quoted):**
- Top-level site navigation includes a dedicated **`PRIVATE LABEL`**, **`ODM`**, **`OEM`** section.
- A `SPICE PACKAGING` page states: *"The same product can be packed in multiple options, including bulk bags, pouches, jars, and retail-ready packaging, with flexible sizes and labeling. This allows our partners to easily adapt products for food service, retail, private label, and international distribution."*
- Product menu includes retail-packaging categories: *Organic Spices Retail Packaging*, *Whole Spices Retail Packaging*, *Spices Powder Retail Packaging*, *Blended Spices Retail Packaging*, *Leaves Retail Packaging*.

**Conclusion:** publicly, Pearl **claims** private label, ODM and OEM capability with bulk, pouch, jar and retail-ready packing options and flexible labelling. That is a **capability claim**, not verified certification of the packing operation.

**What this changes:** Route A (certified overseas finished Mogra units) is **plausible** for Pearl — there is public evidence the capability exists. It does **not** upgrade the organic status of the packing operation, and it does not confirm MOQ or that a Mogra-label unit would be organically certifiable.

## 6. What is safe to state today

- Pearl Group Corporation holds a **current USDA-NOP certification** issued by **Control Union Certifications B.V.**, scope **CROPS + HANDLING**, verified in the public **USDA Organic Integrity Database on 2026-10-02**.
- The certification explicitly covers **organic whole black pepper** and **organic ground black pepper** (observed), plus a broad spice and herb range.
- Pearl publicly **claims** private-label / OEM / ODM and retail packing capability.
- **Turmeric powder** is supplier-claimed but **not yet OID-verified**.

## 7. What must NOT be stated today

- That Pearl can supply **organic turmeric powder** with the same certainty as whole/ground pepper.
- That Pearl's **packing operation** is organically certified.
- That a **Mogra-labelled** product would be eligible for the USDA organic seal.
- Any **price, MOQ, or lead time**.
