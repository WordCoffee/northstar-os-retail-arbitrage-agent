## B. The single most important finding

**If this product is classified as a dietary supplement, Amazon's 2026 supplement policy requires verified cGMP compliance through an Amazon-approved TIC provider — and a standalone COA does not equal Amazon approval.**

That means the **~$800 test may not be sufficient for Amazon**, and the selected laboratory may or may not be an Amazon-approved TIC. Verification, facility-document and label-review costs would sit **outside** the $800.

This is the biggest single risk to the timeline and the budget. **It is unresolved until the classification question (gate G2) is answered.**

## D. What must happen before inventory is released

Release gates **G1–G9** (see `release_gates.csv`). In plain order:

1. **G1** Count, lots, weight, condition, traceability established.
2. **G2** Classification and Amazon path understood.
3. **G3** Lab, panel, sampling and turnaround confirmed.
4. **G4** Results reviewed against specifications by a qualified person.
5. **G5** Shelf-life / date-marking basis accepted — **launch blocker if unresolved**.
6. **G6** Label approved, organic claim supported, Amazon verification complete where applicable.
7. **G7** Pilot unit and carton inspection passed.
8. **G8** Shipment released.
9. **G9** Buyable listing confirmed.

**Stock stays on documented hold until these are met.** Not all 500 bags need to ship in the first shipment; damaged or quarantined units are not sellable.
