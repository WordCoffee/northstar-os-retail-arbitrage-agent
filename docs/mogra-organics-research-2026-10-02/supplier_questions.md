## A. Universal gate questions (ask every supplier, always)

These can end an evaluation immediately. Ask them before anything commercial.

1. Which organic standards do you currently hold — USDA NOP, COR, EU, NPOP, JAS, other?
2. **Certifying body name(s)?**
3. **Certificate number(s)?**
4. **Issue and expiry dates?**
5. **Certificate scope — tick all that apply to your operation:** Grower / Processor / Handler / Repacker / Trader / Exporter / Packer / Private-label packer / Retailer
6. Does your scope cover the **specific activity we need** (i.e. the entity that **exports to the United States**)?
7. If not, which affiliated entity holds that scope — and is it **named on the certificate**?
8. Do you issue an **organic transaction certificate (OTC)** per shipment — automatic, or on request?
9. May we verify your operation in the **USDA Organic Integrity Database**? If so, give the exact **operation name** and **certifier** as registered. If you would rather not, say why.
10. Has any organic certificate you hold ever been **suspended, revoked, or placed on probation**?
11. **Please attach your current certificate(s).** Without this we cannot progress.
12. For **each product form we ask about** (whole vs ground vs powder vs sliced), is that **specific form** covered by your certification? Do not answer at facility level only.

> **Why 12 matters:** a HANDLING scope is a certification scope. It is not proof that every facility, process, product or label is approved. Whole-pepper certification does not automatically mean ground-pepper certification, and fresh turmeric certainly does not mean turmeric powder.

## B. Product-specific questions

### Black pepper
1. Grade(s) and origin (Malabar / Tellicherry / TGEB / Ceylon / light berries / FAQ)?
2. Piperine content and volatile oil (% or g/100g)?
3. Density (g/L)? Moisture % max? Foreign matter % max?
4. Whole, cracked, coarse-ground and fine-ground — which forms are organically covered?
5. Mesh/particle size options for ground?
6. Treatment: none / steam-sterilised / irradiated / other? Is the treatment **compatible with your organic certification** (irradiation is not).

### Turmeric
1. Form(s) — fresh root / dried slices / fingers / powder? **Which are organically certified?**
2. Curcuminoids % (and by what assay method)?
3. Moisture % max? Mesh options for powder?
4. **Adulteration screening** — do you test for lead / lead chromate / metanil yellow or other colorant adulteration? Method and frequency?
5. Lead (ppm max), aflatoxin / mycotoxin (ppb max)?
6. If organic **powder** is claimed, is powder named on your organic certificate or an addendum — or is certification held only for the root/sliced form?

### Herbs (oregano, basil, thyme, rosemary, parsley, dill, sage, bay, mint)
1. Cut size / sieve specification?
2. Foreign matter and moisture % max?
3. Which specific herbs are organically covered?
4. Do you handle the 9 major allergens at the same facility? Segregation and cleaning protocol?

### Cinnamon (**species identity is critical**)
1. Species — *Cinnamomum verum* (Ceylon) vs *cassia* vs *burmannii*? State the species; do not answer "cinnamon".
2. Is species identity **documented by test or supplier declaration** that you can pass on?
3. Coumarin content if applicable?

### Salt / salt-containing blends
1. Confirmed plainly: **plain culinary salt cannot be USDA organic.** Do you agree, and do you claim otherwise anywhere in your materials?
2. Grain size options? Origin?
3. **Full additive declaration** — anticaking agents, free-flowing agents, or none?
4. Food-grade documentation, contaminant testing (heavy metals, microplastics where relevant)?
5. For salt-containing seasoning blends: which components are organic, and how is the blend positioned?

## C. Quality, testing and documentation

1. Do you provide a **lot-specific COA** with each shipment?
2. Which tests does it include — Salmonella, E. coli, coliform, yeast/mould, total plate count, heavy metals, pesticide residues, aflatoxin/mycotoxins, moisture, water activity, allergen control?
3. In-house or third-party lab? **Name and accreditation.**
4. Are results available for the **specific lot we would receive, before shipment**?
5. Do you retain a sample of every shipped lot?
6. Farm- or grower-level traceability? Farm-to-lot?
7. Recall procedure in place?
8. Shelf life at shipment? Storage requirements?

## D. Commercial and logistics

1. **MOQ** per product, per form, **per packaging format** (do not give one blanket MOQ).
2. Price at each tier: **5–25 lb**, **25–100 lb**, **100–500 lb**, **500–2,000 lb** — or your closest equivalents. State currency, Incoterm and named place **per tier**.
3. Is freight, insurance, export documentation, duty or packaging **included or separate**? Itemise.
4. Price validity period? Firm or indicative?
5. Payment terms?
6. Lead time from PO?
7. Packaging: 25 kg PP with liner / 50 kg jute / other? Is packaging organic-contact-safe and food-grade certified?
8. Sample / pre-production sample — cost, lead time, courier?
9. **Origin separation** — state separately: country **grown**, country **processed**, country **packed**, shipping warehouse/export port.
10. Who is the **exporter of record** on documents?
11. Which US import documents do you routinely supply — phytosanitary, certificate of origin, commercial invoice, packing list, B/L, organic transaction certificate, fumigation?
12. HS/HTS code you declare per product?
13. Will you support the **NOP Import Certificate** process, including arranging any accredited certifier review we require?
14. Can you ship DDP to a US 3PL or FBA-prep address? Which Incoterms do you support (FOB/CFR/CIF/DAP/DDP)?
15. Prior US import experience for this product — years and volumes, if you are willing to share?

## E. Private label / co-packing (only where relevant)

1. **Bulk only, blending, co-packing, or turnkey finished units?** Be specific about what you do in-house vs outsource.
2. Is your **packing/repacking facility itself organically certified as a HANDLER/REPACKER** — not merely as a grower or trader? Certificate number and scope.
3. MOQ **per private-label format and per SKU**?
4. Formats — glass jar / PET jar / stand-up pouch / sachet / other, and sizes?
5. Tamper evidence? Lot code and best-before coding?
6. Who generates the barcode — you, us, or a third-party GS1 provider?
7. Who bears **artwork and plate/setup costs**?
8. If we develop a blend formula, **who owns the formula** — buyer, supplier, or joint?
9. Is repackaging under **our brand** permitted, and under what written terms?
10. Sample/pre-production sample process for private label?
