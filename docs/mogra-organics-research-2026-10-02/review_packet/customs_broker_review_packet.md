# Customs-Broker Review Packet — Mogra Organics

**Status:** prepared for a licensed customs broker. **NOT SENT.** No broker has been contacted.
**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Purpose:** give a broker the product and origin information needed to confirm classification, duty, AD/CVD scope and entry requirements. This is **not** a customs determination and contains **no rate assumptions**.

---

## 1. Why this packet exists

No cost figure in the Mogra research includes duty, AD/CVD, or entry fees, because:

- The exact 10-digit HTS lines have not been confirmed.
- Base duty rates were not read from the live USITC HTS.
- **AD/CVD coverage has not been confirmed either way.** AD/CVD is not in the HTS (USITC); it depends on specific orders and their scope. An unsuccessful search does not establish that no order applies.
- Origin-specific additional-tariff measures have been volatile and cannot be assumed.

**Consequence:** every international route is `LANDED_COST_INCOMPLETE`. That is deliberate and should remain so until this review is done.

## 5. Organic import chain — separate from duty

This is **not** the broker's determination, but it interacts with entry:

| Role | Who (proposed) | Obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Exporter / final handler abroad | The overseas supplier | Must hold organic certification covering the product and export activity |
| Certifier abroad | e.g. Control Union | Issues the exporter's organic certificate; issues the NOP Import Certificate |
| **Importer (organic)** | **Mogra or its importer** | **Importer generally must be certified as an organic operation**; the import certificate is matched to the importer's certification |
| FSVP importer | The US importer | Must perform foreign supplier verification |
| Importer of Record | Whoever files entry | Duty liability and record-keeping |

**Open question flagged for the broker and a certifier:** whether Mogra importing organic ingredients triggers a certification obligation for Mogra, and whether that obligation exists **even if Mogra does not apply the USDA seal.**

## 6. What this packet does NOT do

- It does **not** assert that no AD/CVD order applies.
- It does **not** substitute for a formal scope ruling if ambiguity emerges.
- It does **not** authorise any import.
