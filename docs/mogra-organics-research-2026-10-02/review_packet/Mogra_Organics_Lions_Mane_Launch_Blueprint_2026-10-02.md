## 1. Executive decision brief

**What we have:** approximately 500 bags of Lion's mane mushroom powder (owner-reported), stored at the owner's home, described as organic. No testing completed. No Amazon listing.

**The decision this blueprint supports:** whether, when and how this **already-owned inventory** can reach Amazon FBA and become buyable — without spending money on testing that doesn't answer the right questions, and without releasing stock that isn't ready.

**Three findings that change the plan:**

1. **No supporting documentation exists in the project.** I searched the full project tree: there is no lab identity, no test panel, no supplier invoice, no organic certificate, no COA, no bag weight, no lot codes. Everything is owner-reported until evidence arrives.
2. **A two-week test is not a shelf life.** Nothing currently substantiates a date marking. Product age cannot be reset to the testing date.
3. **If this is a dietary supplement, Amazon's 2026 policy requires verified cGMP via an Amazon-approved TIC provider — and a standalone COA does not equal Amazon approval.** The ~$800 test may not be sufficient, and the chosen lab may not be an approved TIC. This is the largest single risk to timeline and budget.

**Earliest realistic buyable:** **~18 Nov (fast)**, ~**23 Nov–1 Dec (base)**, **1 Dec or later (delay)**. None is guaranteed.

**Immediate blocker set:** classification (G2), lab/panel confirmation (G3), shelf-life basis (G5).

## 4. Shelf-life evidence gate

Four things that must not be conflated:

| Type | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **A. Current-condition testing** | Is the stock now acceptable? |
| **B. Identity / composition testing** | Is it what it claims to be? |
| **C. Safety / contaminant testing** | Is it safe to sell? |
| **D. Shelf-life / date-marking evidence** | How long is it defensibly good, and what date may we print? |

**The quoted work most likely addresses A and C.** Nothing currently addresses **D**.

Inputs required for D: actual manufacture date · supplier stability evidence · exact formulation · packaging barrier and seal · storage history · real-time or accelerated study evidence · remaining shelf life at Amazon check-in.

**Rules:**
- Do **not** reset product age to the testing date.
- Do **not** invent or extend an expiration date to satisfy Amazon.
- **If D remains unresolved, it is a launch blocker** with a specific closure action.

## 5. Food versus supplement pathway

**Unresolved.** Classification depends on the **actual label, directions, intended use, ingredients and claims** — not on the fact that it is a mushroom powder. Some mushroom products are conventional foods, some are supplements. **A category must not be chosen to avoid requirements.**

If **conventional food**: Nutrition Facts or a valid exemption; permitted claims are limited; no disease or structure/function claims; food labelling and facility rules apply.

If **dietary supplement**: Supplement Facts; structure/function claims require substantiation and a disclaimer; **Amazon's 2026 supplement policy requires verified cGMP through an Amazon-approved TIC provider**.

**Critical separation:** the **~$800 test is distinct** from any additional Amazon-authorised TIC validation, facility-document review, label review, or retesting. Those costs sit outside it and are currently unknown.

**Not assumed:** seller-account readiness, category eligibility, Brand Registry, product identifiers, or compliance approval. None is confirmed.

## 6. Organic and labelling readiness

**Organic is owner-reported.** Laboratory testing does **not** prove organic certification.

Required to inspect: supplier operation and product coverage · manufacturing/packing chain · the exact finished-product organic claim · whether any USDA seal or other mark applies.

**Label-gap review for this exact SKU** must cover: product identity · net quantity · ingredient declaration · responsible business information · the appropriate facts panel or exemption basis · directions and warnings · organic wording and marks · lot/date marking · barcode and Amazon-label placement · claims that appear on **both** package and listing.

**Prohibited without lawful, substantiated basis:** disease-treatment claims, guaranteed cognitive performance, "lead-free", "FDA-approved", superiority claims.

**If bags need only external labelling** → a labelling workflow.
**If bags must be opened, resealed or repacked** → a **separate qualified packing task**, and it may affect certification.

**Label approval is owner-reported; its scope is NOT audited by this research.**

## 8. FBA preparation and release

Sequence — each state is distinct and later than the one before:

```
TESTS RECEIVED → RESULTS ACCEPTED → COMPLIANCE READY → PACKAGING READY →
DISPATCHED → DELIVERED → RECEIVED / AVAILABLE → BUYABLE
```

**A laboratory report is not automatic authorisation to ship.**

Pre-release checks: unit seal and packaging inspection · product/expiration attributes at ASIN creation · supported date marking · **sufficient remaining shelf life** · barcode/FNSKU · label placement not covering mandatory information · case packing and carton labels · separate handling of different lots/date codes where required · shipment-plan inputs · transit and receiving uncertainty · listing buyability check.

**Do not dispatch quarantined inventory. Do not assume all units must go in the first shipment.**
