## Evaluation rule — apply before ranking, not after

**Organic eligibility is a gate, not a score.**

1. **Gate 1 — Organic product evidence.** Verified certificate, current, scope
   covering the activity performed, product covered. Marketing claims, logos
   and directory listings fail this gate.
2. **Gate 2 — Credible food-grade supply capability.** Documented food-grade
   suitability, COA availability, traceability.
3. **Gate 3 — Plausible commercial route.** MOQ reachable, account/application
   path realistic, incoterm stated.
4. **Gate 4 — No unresolved certification contradiction.** Any suspension,
   revocation, scope mismatch or expiry problem that is not resolved disqualifies.

**Only leads passing all four gates are ranked.** Among those:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Comparable landed cost | 35% |
| Quality / specification evidence | 25% |
| Pilot MOQ and account accessibility | 15% |
| Organic traceability and documentation | 15% |
| Reliability, replenishment, packaging fit | 10% |

**Suppliers who only quote on request stay unranked on cost.** Unknown cost is
not cheap cost. Show provisional strengths and the missing inputs instead, and
mark `PRICING_INCOMPLETE` / `LANDED_COST_INCOMPLETE`.

Country is not a price or quality proxy.
