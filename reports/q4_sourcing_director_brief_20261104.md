Generated: 2026-11-04 · Zero-gating enforced · Turnkey only
Assumptions: 15% referral · label/prep $0.15 · inbound freight $0.25 · MOQ ≤500 · test 100–300

**The arithmetic that governs every row:**
> `Net = Retail − Wholesale − (0.15 × Retail) − FBA fee − $0.40`

At **$19.99** you can afford **$7.49 wholesale** for a $5 net.
At **$16.99** you can afford only **$4.94**.
At **$14.99** — **$3.94**.

That is why almost every viable row below prices at $16.99–$19.99, not $14.99.

# SECTION D — PRINTER & LABEL ECONOMICS

## Current setup: Canon PIXMA TR4720

| Line | Cost |
|---|---|
| Avery/OnlineLabels 6-up sheet | ~$0.45 |
| **Per label** | **$0.075** |
| Ink (XL colour, ~$0.08/page ÷ 6) | $0.013 |
| **Total per label** | **~$0.09** |
| Print time, 50 sheets @ 2 min | **~100 minutes** |

**Verdict: adequate, not optimal.**

✅ Fine for **100–300 colour brand labels** — $27 in materials, one afternoon.
❌ Fails at 1,000+ units (ink cost and time both scale badly).
❌ **Inkjet ink is water-soluble.** On bath salts, dishcloths or kitchen goods, a damp hand smears the label. That is a returns driver.

## Where the TR4720 actually breaks: FNSKU barcodes

Barcodes must scan at FC receiving. Inkjet barcodes on gloss stock are the single most common avoidable failure I flagged across this whole project. **Do not print FNSKU on the TR4720.**

## Secondary printer recommendation

| Option | Price | Cost/label | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thermal — Rollo X1040 / Zebra ZD220** | **$180–$250** | **~$0.02** | ✅ **Buy this first** |
| Epson EcoTank ET-2850 | $250–$300 | ~$0.02 colour | ◐ Only if volume exceeds 1,000 |
| Brother colour laser | $250–$350 | $0.15–$0.25 | ❌ Toner kills the margin |
| Outsourced roll labels | $0.06–$0.10 | ✅ | Best above 500 units |

## The verdict

**Keep the TR4720 for colour brand labels. Add a thermal printer (~$200) for FNSKU and shipping labels.**

That combination costs about **$0.11 per unit all-in** and removes the two failure modes — smeared ink and unscannable barcodes. Skip the colour laser entirely; at 100–300 units it cannot pay back its toner.

**Do not buy anything else until you've shipped one test run.** The thermal printer is a real asset because it's used forever. A second inkjet is not.
