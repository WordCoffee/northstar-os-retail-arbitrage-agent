# WHAT WILL BREAK THIS

1. **Bulk-only suppliers.** 8 of the 20 Mogro suppliers cannot sticker. Do not spend time on them for this launch.
2. **Label transit.** 2–3 days you cannot spare. Prefer suppliers who print in-house (Adagio, Sugar 'N Spice).
3. **FNSKU barcode quality.** Print one sample, scan it with a phone, before ordering 2,750.
4. **No demand validation.** These six are margin-verified, not demand-verified. Amazon was not scanned — the first 60 days of sell-through is the real test.
5. **Supplier sticker refusal.** Expect ~1 in 3 to decline. Call before ordering labels.
