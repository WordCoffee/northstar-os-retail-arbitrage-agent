## 1. PLAN

### Phase 0 — Verify the powder (before spending anything)

The entire plan depends on four documents. Get them before ordering packaging.

| # | Document | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | USDA organic certificate + certifier name | Without it the bag cannot carry the organic seal |
| 2 | Organic Transaction Certificate (OTC) for the lot | The co-packer needs this to keep their handler certification |
| 3 | COA — beta-glucan %, moisture, heavy metals, micro | The only real potency evidence |
| 4 | Ingredient declaration | Fruiting body only, or myceliated grain? |

**Gate 1 — if no organic certificate: STOP.** Either reposition as non-organic or find certified powder.
**Gate 2 — if myceliated grain: REPRICE to $19.99 and label honestly.**

Cost: $0. Duration: 3–10 days.

### Phase 1 — Spec lock and supplier selection (week 1)

**Pouch spec (this exact string goes to the converter):**

**4.6" width is mandatory.** Nutra Connection's floor is 4.5"; a standard 6 oz pouch at 4.3" will be rejected.

**Gate 3 — co-packer must confirm in writing: (a) 2,500 units, (b) customer-supplied pouches, (c) their organic handler certificate number.**

**Gate 4 — proof approved physically, not on screen.**

### Phase 3 — Commit orders (week 2)

**Gate 5 — written reorder price.** If $5/kg is clearance, the margin model changes materially.

### Phase 4 — Filling (weeks 3–6)

Cost: fill $1,375 + setup $300. Duration: 1–3 weeks depending on scheduling.

### Phase 5 — Inbound to FBA (weeks 6–7)

- Carton labelling, Amazon-partnered carrier
- Ship 2,500 units

Cost: ~$900. Duration: 5–10 days.

### Phase 6 — Listing and launch

- Price at $24.99 recommended (see §3)
