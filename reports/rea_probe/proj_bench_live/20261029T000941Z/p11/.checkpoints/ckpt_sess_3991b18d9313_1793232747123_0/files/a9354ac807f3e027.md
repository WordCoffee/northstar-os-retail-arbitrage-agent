2. **Prepress Validation**: Run a prepress check to verify color profiles, bleed, and trim marks; confirm the file is print-ready with no embedded fonts or unsupported elements.
3. **OCR Verification**: Perform an OCR scan on the final rendered label to ensure all required terms are correctly recognized and legible.
4. **Resolution Check**: Analyze the final output to confirm that any residual artifacts or anti-aliasing errors are under 0.5 px.
5. **Final Approval**: Sign off on the label as printable and ready for production after passing all checks.
