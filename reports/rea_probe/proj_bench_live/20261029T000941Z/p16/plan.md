1. **Scope Lanes**: Define distinct inspection and browsing lanes with specific entry/exit criteria to ensure clear boundaries.
2. **Implement Inspection Lane**: Execute automated checks or manual reviews within the scoped lane to identify potential issues.
3. **Insert Human Gate**: Pause the workflow to require explicit human approval before transitioning from inspection to browsing.
4. **Execute Browsing Lane**: Proceed with the browsing tasks only after the gate is cleared, adhering to the defined scope.
5. **Verify Compliance**: Confirm that the human gate was triggered and that both lanes operated within their defined scopes.
