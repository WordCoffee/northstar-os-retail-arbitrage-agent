1. **Design & Layout**: Create the label artwork on the measured pouch plate, ensuring all text and graphics are vector-based and positioned within the safe print area.
2. **Prepress Verification**: Run a preflight check to confirm color modes (CMYK), bleed settings, and font embedding; verify that the residual error between the design bounds and the physical plate measurement is under 0.5 px.
3. **OCR Validation**: Generate a high-resolution raster proof and run OCR to confirm that all required terms are accurately readable and match the source data.
4. **Printability Check**: Simulate the print process to ensure the file is printable, checking for ink coverage limits and resolution integrity.
5. **Final Sign-off**: Archive the verified file and confirm the label is ready for production based on the passed residual, OCR, and printability checks.
