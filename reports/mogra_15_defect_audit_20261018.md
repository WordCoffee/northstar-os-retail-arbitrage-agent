# MOGRA 15-Point Defect Audit — f/g-series review

_Findings from the f01-f10 and g01-g10 flat art + mockups_

## Layout & composition (5)
1. **All fronts share one composition** — logo top-center, headline center, legend bottom on ~8 of 10. Same visual rhythm, no shelf differentiation.
2. **Identical facts line on every design** — "170 G · 34 SERVINGS · 5 G PER SCOOP" repeated verbatim; feels templated, not designed.
3. **Dead white space** — large empty zones on most fronts (e.g. bottom third of several), not intentional negative space.
4. **Botanical motif too small** — mushroom engraving reads as a postage-stamp decoration, not hero imagery.
5. **No element reaches the edge** — every design keeps a safe margin, so nothing feels bold or confident.

## Typography & type hierarchy (4)
6. **Front type too small for shelf** — headlines at 8–12mm don't read at arm's length; need 14–20mm display type.
7. **Generic font pairing** — Cormorant Garamond + Inter is the safe default; no distinctive voice.
8. **No display hierarchy on backs** — Supplement Facts box and fine print carry equal visual weight.
9. **Tracking overused** — wide letterspacing on body text reduces readability at small sizes.

## Color & premium feel (3)
10. **Muted, muddy colors** — natural tones read as flat/dirty rather than premium; insufficient contrast between ink and ground.
11. **No texture variance** — digitally flat; no paper grain, no emboss, no foil cue.
12. **Washed-out back logo** — cream wordmark block on dark grounds is unreadable; brand name disappears.

## Logo & branding (3)
13. **Logo underused on front** — tiny incidental mark rather than a brand device; not the visual anchor.
14. **Logo not visible on back** — small square tucked mid-right, grouped with fine print instead of leading the section.
15. **Backs are clones** — identical layout on all 10; only bag color changes. No back differentiation at all.

## Recommended fix
- Redo all 10 fronts with 100% unique compositions (vertical type, diagonal splits, off-edge type, giant logo, etc.)
- Make the back logo **35–40mm**, placed at top with the About Lion's Mane section, high-contrast wordmark (not icon-only)
- Export **flat, one folder** — front+back per design, no subfolders, no sheets
