## 1 · Turn contract v2
`TurnIn` gains `media` (image|video), `thinking` (off|standard|deep), `style`
(concise|balanced|detailed) and `attachments` (≤12 ids), all pattern-validated. The
switches reach the model as a **separate system directive built after history**, so
the operator's own words remain exactly what the transcript stores — asserted. The
`start` frame reports the options; a v1 caller is unchanged.

## 2 · Duplex voice loop
- **Server (tested):** `POST /api/chat/voice/speak` synthesises one line through the
  existing voice-studio adapter. Unreachable → **503 with the real reason**; a reply
  with no `audio_url` → **502** (never a silent success).
- **Client (build-verified):** a Talk toggle runs listen → local whisper transcript →
  turn → speak → listen. An energy VAD ends capture after speech followed by ~900 ms
  of quiet (15 s ceiling). **Barge-in**: while Bojo speaks, mic energy above
  threshold pauses the audio and resumes capture immediately.
- **Honest gap:** the client loop is not functionally tested — this environment has
  no microphone, and stubbing `getUserMedia`/`MediaRecorder` deserves its own
  Playwright harness. Treat it as "wired, needs a mic harness", not "proven".
- **Dependency:** speaking requires the voice-studio engine on `127.0.0.1:8321`.
  When it is down the loop says so (`Bojo can't speak right now: …`) and keeps
  working in text — it never pretends to talk.

## 3 · Uploads
`POST /api/design-studio/assets/upload` stores bytes in `data/studio_generated/` and
registers a real asset, so an attachment becomes an asset id that renders through the
existing `/assets/{id}/file` route. Tested: mime allow-list, 25 MB cap, basename-only
storage (traversal asserted impossible), neutralised filenames, extension from mime,
and a refused upload writes nothing. The composer button sends the CSRF double-submit
header and shows removable chips.

## 5 · Paid execution
`creative_execute.execute()`: confirm → **reserve** → generate → **commit**, with
**release** on any failure. Order is the contract: a failed render costs nothing; no
credits means the generator is never called (no free work); a replay never
double-charges. The receipt carries the real model cost and the credits charged. The
`creative_plan` tool now executes draft/final and returns the real asset ids.
