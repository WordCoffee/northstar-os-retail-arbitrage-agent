## 5. WHAT "UNRESTRICTED" DOES AND DOES NOT MEAN

**Confirmed unrestricted (no Amazon approval needed to list):**
- Home & kitchen goods, bath accessories, candles
- Seeds and garden kits (non-noxious varieties)
- Stationery, journals, planners, cards, posters, stickers
- Tote bags, apparel (standard, no trademark issues)

**Excluded from this list on purpose (would need Amazon ungating):**
- Dietary supplements / herbal capsules — gated
- Topical acne / sunscreen / drug-claim cosmetics — gated
- Pesticides, fungicides, insecticides — restricted
- Medical devices, thermometers, PPE — restricted
- Alcohol, tobacco, CBD, vape — restricted
- Baby formula, contact lenses, laser products — restricted
- Anything hazmat/flammable aerosol — restricted

**Residual legal duties that remain regardless:**
- **MoCRA** — all Mogra cosmetics need Responsible Person, registration, INCI, safety file
- **FTC textile rule** — origin + fiber content on totes/tees
- **Seed laws** — noxious-weed list per state
- **CPSIA** — if any item is marketed to children under 12
