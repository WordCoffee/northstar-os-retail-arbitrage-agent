## The tool-offering question, answered

The order asked: was the work lost in a merge, or was the "44 wired, 0 declared" claim wrong?

**Neither.** The handlers are wired — `scan_products` and `watch_competitor` reach production
code and return their own refusals. The defect was one level up: `_bojo_handler` forwards the
chat spec's args straight into `registry.call(name, **args)`, and six specs used a friendlier
shape than the handler accepts (`product` vs `sku`, `facts` vs `inputs`, `budget` vs
`daily_budget`, `asin` vs `asins`, `template`/`format` vs `template_id`). Every call answered
**"bad arguments"** while the tool was listed as offered.

After the fix, all seven answer with their own reasons and none says "bad arguments";
`plan_campaign` returns ok=true. `test_tool_offering.py` no longer carries a hand-maintained
list of seven names — it derives the invariant from the code (a forwarded spec may only offer
parameters its handler accepts, and must offer every required one). 11 tests, 107 in the bojo
suites.
