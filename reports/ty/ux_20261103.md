## 4. Decisions

All ~40 decisions are in `reports/ty/ux_decisions.md`. The ones that shape the product:

- **The canvas is the only place that edits the canvas.** `canvas_edit` checks that an instruction is
  satisfiable and never mutates; the browser applies it. One implementation, so the two cannot drift.
- **An unambiguous canvas sentence is applied locally before the model is called.** The pass
  criterion was a canvas that actually changes; waiting for a model tool call makes the feature
  unusable when the lane is down (it was down during U8).
- **The dieline overlay is DOM-only.** It cannot reach an export, proven by byte-identical PNGs with
  it on and off, and its geometry comes from the same builder as the print lane.
- **The 13 catalogue-only services get no invented pages.** Nine of them have no route; a fake
  workspace would be fabrication, so the notice distinguishes a catalogue entry from an app route.
- **Depth modes change the record limit and say so.** "Deep research" cannot be a claim about quality
  over a keyword index.
- **The nova is rate-limited to one a minute and skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`.** A reward
  that fires every time is noise; motion the user asked not to see is not a reward.
- **The `as`-prop pattern is legitimate React.** The JSX guard flagged it as a crash; the guard was
  fixed, not the component, and the fix is pinned by a two-sided test.

**Needs you** (`reports/ty/needs_you.md`): does the Design Studio keep its own chat box, or do the
three stale chat specs move to `/ui/workbench` (the default if you stay silent)? Also open there: the
tailnet proxy that blocks `dev-start.ps1`, the studio top-up processor, and the media lane arming
(`MEDIA_GEN_LIVE`) that the asset-dashboard test waits on.

**Token use:** ~1.6 M of the 5 M cap (estimates, chars/4 — not billing figures).

## 5. What to look at first

1. **`/ui/workbench`** — open Services, add three, switch between them: the theme, the scene and the
   workspace swap while Bojo stays docked. This is the whole idea in one screen.
2. **Design Studio → Canvas** — add a headline, add an image, drag it, undo, export PNG and PDF, then
   reload: it comes back. Then say "make the headline bigger" to Bojo.
3. **Design Studio → Dielines & Print** — pick a doypack, set dimensions, turn the overlay on, and
   export. The guides stay on screen and out of the file, which is the point.

   change by another session and fails with `KeyError: 'app'`. I did not touch it.
4. **The studio scene runs ~45 fps against a 60 budget.** The recorded next lever is baking the
   full-screen backdrop into one offscreen image; caching the planet disc alone changed nothing.
5. **The visual baseline is on disk, not in git** (`.gitignore` excludes `reports/**/*.png`). The
   committed record is `manifest.json` with sizes and sha256 per shot.
6. **The packaging lane is not Pacdora/Packify-scale.** One parametric builder, four structures
   (pouch, carton, bottle, tube), draft geometry that a printer must confirm; no template catalogue
   and no DXF/AI output.
