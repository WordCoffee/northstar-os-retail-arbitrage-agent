## The layering defect, and what it actually was

`simple.css` had grown by appending override blocks, so the same surface was
defined three or four times: `.chat-empty` at lines 279/712/920 and
`.chat-suggestions` at 287/509/719. The winner depended on source order and on
the active vista design. Two separate symptoms came from that:

1. **The greeting heading was invisible on load** — not hidden, *scrolled out*.
   Measured: the stream was `clientHeight 239` against `scrollHeight 267`, and
   the auto-pin effect scrolled to the bottom even with zero messages, so the
   43px heading sat above the fold. Fixed twice over: the empty state no longer
   overflows (197px in 239px), and the stream refuses to auto-pin while there
   are no messages.
2. **The starter prompts rendered 2+1** — `simple.css:719` re-declared the grid
   after the component style, so three cards wrapped 2-then-1. Now three across,
   one column under 1000px.

Root fix: a new `styles/canonical.css`, loaded after `simple.css` and
`simple-designs.css`, that owns this surface with attribute-selector specificity
(`.mb-shell[data-simple-design]`) so per-design overrides cannot silently win
again. Nothing above it was deleted (other surfaces still rely on those rules).

## New scorecard dimensions

- **service-coverage** — ten fixed service routes; records render ok, ms,
  heading, page errors and console errors with samples.
- **tab-state** — the workbench tab bar exists and survives switching. Measured
  honestly: `wb-tabbar` is **absent** on `/ui/workbench`, so tab state cannot be
  measured yet. The gate is "measured state matches the declared expectation"
  (`TABS_EXPECTED = false`), which fails the day someone flips it to true — not a
  silent skip.
- **scene-readability** — samples panel text, composites the alpha chain
  properly (a raw `rgba()` read made a 5% white card look near-white), and
  reports worst-case contrast plus whether the panel is translucent. This is
  what caught the 3.38:1 chips.

## Cycle 7 (same day) - creative: identity preserved by construction, proven on a real scene

`tools/experiment_composite.py` renders an empty scene with FLUX 3 (27.3 s),
cuts the seller's real product out with BiRefNet (9.7 s), and places it with
`product_composite` - no download, no licence, no spend.

**Result** (`renders/autonomy_probe/composite/lifestyle_linen_composite.png`):
the seller's own pouch - MOGRA ORGANICS, LION'S MANE, "100% PURE - NO FILLERS",
"NET WT. 6 oz (170 g)" - legible and unaltered, standing on a linen surface in
window light. That is the tryholo-class capability the research said we lacked,
and it needed zero new weights.

Two defects found and fixed in the same pass:

1. **The product was sliced by the bottom edge** (anchor 0.72 on a 716px pouch
   put its base at y=1024). `place_product` now keeps a 2% frame margin.
2. **The identity check compared scene pixels with the product** and reported
   0.93 "drifted" for a correct composite. It now masks by the cutout's alpha,
   so it measures the product only.

Still open, measured: `picture_scorecard.product_fidelity` scores 0.0 on this
composite because its blind `subject_bbox` picks the wall/table boundary instead
of the pouch. The composite is correct; the automatic detector is not. The next
pass must either accept a known product box or use the alpha region.

**Blocked, logged:** a second scene render was refused with
`system_ram_insufficient: free 3830 MB, need 4096 MB`. The lane is correct to
refuse; the box needs ~300 MB more headroom (a ComfyUI restart is the usual way).
