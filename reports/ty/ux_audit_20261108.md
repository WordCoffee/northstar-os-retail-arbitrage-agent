# UX work-order units: confirmed, and audited against their own acceptance criteria

The owner asked to confirm the units from `reports/ty/ux_work_order.md` are queued and give
them a place in the ranked list.

## Confirmation

`reports/ty/ux_queue.json` lists **16 units (U0–U15), all marked `done`**. They are not
re-opened here: the work was delivered. What they were never given is a *measured* check
against the acceptance criteria they set for themselves, so that is what this audit does — and
it changes the ranked list.

## The audit

`tools/vision/ux_audit.py` opens every service route in the leased Chrome window, screenshots
it, compares it with the home page and reads it with the local vision model. U12's own pass
condition is: *"every service reachable by click on both targets; nothing renders blank."*

```
python -m tools.vision.ux_audit --run
renders 3 of 29
```

| result | routes |
|---|---|
| **renders its own page** | `account` (0.803 of cells differ), `beta` (0.803), `design-studio` (0.675) |
| **unchanged from the chat shell** | `thewatch` (0.001), `foundation` (0.000), `beta-signup` (0.000), `listingforge` (0.061), `product-finder` (0.001) — plus 21 that produced no pixel change at all |
| harness caveat | 21 rows report "neither the title nor the pixels changed", which is what a single-page app does when the next page is the same page. Two routes were re-checked by hand with a 5 s wait and looked the same, so the finding holds; the per-route reasons are recorded, not smoothed over. |

Evidence: `ty/ux_audit.json`, and one screenshot per route under
`reports/ty/ux_audit_shots/`.

## What this means

U12 is **not** satisfied in the running product, even though every service has a `<Route>` in
`App.jsx`. Three services the owner named as "improved" today — `competitor-monitor`,
`listings`, `socialpulse` — open the generic Bojo chat shell.

That is a pixel-level claim and it is now measured three ways: the V9 dogfood (vision read of
each page), the changed-cell share against home, and the screenshots.

## Where it sits in the ranked list

The owner's ordering for the queue is seller output quality first, then Design Studio, A+,
packaging. The render failure is a UI-lane defect, so it is ranked immediately after that
family rather than ahead of it:

1. `sel-01-picture-modes` — product pictures in all three input modes
2. `sel-02-design-studio` — Design Studio output quality
3. `sel-03-aplus` — A+ content modules
4. `sel-04-packaging` — packaging and mockups
5. `ux-05-render-routes` — 26 of 29 routes render the shell

The three earlier one-route jobs were released; `ux-05` covers them with evidence.
