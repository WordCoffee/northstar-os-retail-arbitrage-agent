## Cross-Cutting Notes (apply to all three)

### Branding / labeling path (shared)
- Suppliers ship **pre-packaged, unbranded primary packaging**. Mogro branding enters ONLY
  via an exterior sticker (label) + FNSKU at a US prep center — no co-packing, no primary
  re-label inside the supply chain.
- **UPC strategy:** Mogro owns UPC (GS1 US prefix); exterior sticker carries Mogro UPC;
  prep center overlays/assigns FNSKU (Amazon scans FNSKU; UPC visible but harmless).
- **Compliance:** ingredient statement, country-of-origin ("Product of USA" for seed/
  cotton, "Made in USA" for tea tin), and a recyclable-material disclosure affixed as part
  of the exterior sticker where the supplier did not pre-print.

### Cost assumptions (standardized)
- `Sticker/prep`: $0.35–$0.40 per unit (exterior overlay + FNSKU labor/material).
- `Inbound freight`: bundled into the above; final leg via Amazon-partnered carrier
  (fees netted within FBA tier).
- `Referral fee`: 15 % of retail across all three categories.
- `FBA fees`: 2026 published standard-size tier — small/$4.80, medium/$6.50,
  small-light/$4.20.

  exterior sticker (Gallant fabric may be imported, assembled US); handle via sticker.
- **Tea** (Product 3): "herbal" = must be labeled caffeine-free; Adagio organic-herbal
  supply is verified organic — confirm certificate on first PO.
