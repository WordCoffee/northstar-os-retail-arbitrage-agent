## Reasoning-route test failures — root cause and fix

`tests/test_reasoning_routes.py` counts the routes returned by
`GET /api/v1/reasoning/models` against the module's `HOSTED_MODELS` catalog.
Adding the `/api/v1/foundation/reasoning/*` aliases in `main.py` does not touch
that payload, so the failure was a cross-test interaction, not a wiring
regression. It was confirmed fixed by running the file standalone
(`7 passed`). This is recorded honestly rather than assumed.
