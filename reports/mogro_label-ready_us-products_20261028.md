## 5. COMPLIANCE OVERLAY — critical for candidates 1–4

Bath/body products are **cosmetics under FDA MoCRA**, not supplements. Before listing:

1. **Responsible Person + facility registration** — MoCRA requires a US Responsible Person
   and facility registration; the *brand* (Mogro/T2 Holdings) may be the Responsible Person
   even though Bulk Apothecary manufactures. **Confirm in writing who holds this.**
2. **Ingredient declaration** — the exterior label must carry the full INCI ingredient list,
   net contents, and business name/address. Bulk Apothecary supplies the formula sheet;
   our sticker must reproduce it exactly.
3. **Safety substantiation** — MoCRA requires a safety substantiation file. Ask Bulk
   Apothecary whether they provide a shared safety assessment for each base formula
   (most ready-to-label suppliers do; get it in writing).
4. **Amazon cosmetic listing requirements** — Amazon requires ingredient listing and
   may request images of the label; keep the label legible at 2000 px.

**Candidate 5 (loofah)** is a non-cosmetic bath accessory — no MoCRA burden. It is the
lowest-compliance-risk addition of the five.
