Four defects were found and fixed in this lane by testing it against real studio output:

| Defect | What it looked like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `local_voice.synthesize()` returns WAV **bytes**, not a path and no `ok` flag | a successful narration was reported `speech_engine_failed` | read the real contract, write the bytes, reject a non-RIFF payload |

| ASR on a synthetic voice | captions reading “Magra Organics lions main … sir a spoonful” | `align_captions()`: measured timings + script words when the counts match, transcript + a stated mismatch when they do not |

Narration shorter than the clip is padded (`apad`) rather than truncating the video — a
plain `-shortest` would have cut a 15 s master to 10 s.

### 1.2 The 30 s master — the first real long-chain failure, fixed at the cause

| Attempt | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | 12 clips, **1713.7 s** (28.5 min). Picture gate **refused**: `layout_unbalanced` — “vertically cropped and off-center; **hand intrudes into frame**”. The prompt asks for a plain pouch; the hand was invented. design_alignment and typography passed, OCR found no lettering. |
| 2 (re-anchored) | 12 clips, **1568.4 s** (26.1 min). **200 OK.** 30.000 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · **900 frames** · 17,055,572 B · OCR 0 boxes. A frame decoded at 27 s shows a clean, centred kraft pouch — no hand, no drift. |

That is not bad luck, it is the design: every clip chained from the previous clip's last
frame, so error compounds over 12 calls. Fixed properly instead of re-rolling the seed:

* `shorts.generate_stitched(..., reanchor_every=N)` restarts the chain from the original
  plate every N clips;
* the endpoint applies it automatically for masters longer than 8 clips
  (`reanchor_every=4`), leaving short clips byte-identically unchanged;
* two tests pin the behaviour — with re-anchoring the source fed back in is the plate,
  without it the chain never returns to the plate.

Attempt 1 is kept as `reports/v3_master_video_30s_attempt1_drift.json`.

## 4. V3.1 — observation inside the controller (decided, not executed)

State of play, verified by reading the controller (not by assuming):

* the controller's observe-act planner already receives **DOM text** (`page.text`,
  truncated to 2 000 chars) and may propose `screenshot <path>`, `read`, `probe`,
  `console`, `diagnose`;
* it is **off by default** (`CHROMECONNECT_OBSERVE_ACT=1`) and every proposal is validated
  against the policy gate before execution;
* what does **not** exist is the screenshot being fed back into the vision lane inside the
  loop — observation is text-only, and the richer pruned a11y snapshot
  (`Northstar_backend/chromeconnect_observation.py`) is not what the live controller uses.

**Decision:** not executed in this pass, deliberately. That change lands in the browser
controller — the enforcement point the parity harness exists to protect — and it cannot be
verified here end-to-end (live browser + vision model + policy gate) inside this session.
Shipping an unverified change to the guardrail path is exactly what the constitution
forbids. The change is small and specified:

1. in `observe-act-planner.proposeNextStep`, accept an optional screenshot buffer and pass
   it to a local vision call (`vision_verify.verify`) with the task as the constraint;
2. include the verdict as one line of observation text, never as an instruction;
3. keep it behind the existing flag and add it to the parity harness as a seventh point
   before it is enabled by default.

Until then: the vision layer observes (`vision_verify`, 18/20 bake-off) and Chrome Connect
enforces — which is exactly the §5 boundary the V3 readiness report set.
