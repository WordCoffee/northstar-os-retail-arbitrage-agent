# PART 1 — EXECUTIVE BRIEFING & CAPITAL FORECAST

## The strategy in one paragraph

We are not manufacturing. We are buying finished, retail-ready inventory that is already sitting in US warehouses, applying our brand and FNSKU labels at the supplier or a regional prep centre, and routing direct to Amazon FBA. This removes the 6–12 week co-packing cycle entirely and compresses the timeline to **23 days**. The trade-off is honest: we cannot customise the product, only the presentation. That is acceptable for a Q4 test.

## Why 23 days is achievable

| Traditional private label | This sprint |
|---|---|
| 6–12 weeks (formulation, blending, testing) | 23 days |
| MOQ 1,000–5,000 units | 250–500 units |
| Custom packaging tooling | Stock packaging + label |
| Capital $15k–$50k | Capital ~$5k |
| Risk: unsellable inventory | Risk: capped at 250 units/SKU |

# PART 4 — RISK & MITIGATION MATRIX

| # | Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gated Amazon data** — no BSR, review count or search volume available; demand is inferred | **High** | Cap initial inventory at **250 units per SKU**. Lean on Etsy F/W 2026, Walmart Holiday Report and Faire velocity. Treat launch as a paid test, not a forecast. |
| 2 | **Label transit bottleneck** — 2–3 days we do not have | **High** | Restrict vendor selection to suppliers with **in-house or <3-day** printing: **Adagio, PrintPPS, Vervante, Sugar 'N Spice**. Reject any supplier that requires shipped labels. |
| 3 | **Bulk-only suppliers** — will sell material but not sticker | **High** | Explicitly exclude **Mountain Rose, Starwest, Frontier, Monterey Bay, SF Herb, Spicy Organic, Organic Spices, Vanns** from the Q4 sprint. They are January suppliers. |
| 4 | **FNSKU barcode fails to scan** at FC receiving | **Critical** | Print a **single physical sample** and scan it with a phone before committing 550 labels. We supply the FNSKU — the barcode quality is our liability, not the supplier's. |
| 5 | **Supplier refuses to sticker** sealed retail units | Medium | Expect ~1 in 3 to decline. Confirm in writing **before** ordering labels. Fallback: regional prep centre (+2 days, +$0.60/unit). |
| 6 | **Black Friday missed** — 25 Nov is 2 days before the peak | Medium | Amazon receiving runs 7–10 days in late November. Ship by **16 Nov** to guarantee sellable status. If a supplier slips past 6 Nov, switch supplier — not the date. |
| 7 | **Price position mismatch** — e.g. 3-wick candle at $34.99 vs $19.95 market median | Medium | Do not lead with the candle. Lead with ritual gifts where our price sits at market, not above it. |
| 8 | **Organic claim unverifiable** on Mogro food SKUs | Medium | Request organic certificate + transaction certificate before listing. Applies to tea and spice specifically. |
