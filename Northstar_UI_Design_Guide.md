# NORTH STAR — UI DESIGN GUIDE
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Purpose:** Frontend development reference for the North Star OS and AutothinK websites.

---

## 1. NORTH STAR OS: THE AMAZON COMMAND CENTER

### Design A: "THE ANALYST’S DESK"
**Philosophy:** High-end physical workspace meets digital precision. A "Craftsman" aesthetic that feels premium, tactile, and professional.

*   **Theme:** Neo-Skeuomorphic (Physical textures + Digital glow).
*   **Background:** Dark slate or charcoal texture (feels like a high-end desk pad or leather).
*   **Primary Panels:** "Physical" materials like brushed aluminum (sidebar) and digital "leather" (main card borders).
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   **Profit/Success:** Amber / Gold (#D4AF37).
    *   **Navigation/Primary:** Steel Blue (#4682B4).
    *   **Critical Alerts:** Deep Crimson / "Red Wax" (#8B0000).
    *   **Background:** Charcoal (#1A1A1A).
*   **Typography:** 
    *   **Headers:** *Playfair Display* or *Merriweather* (Premium, elegant).
    *   **Data/Body:** *Roboto Mono* or *IBM Plex Mono* (Clean, technical).

#### Layout Architecture
*   **Top Navigation (The "Ribbon"):** Gold-accented bar showing Total Profit, BSR Health, and the "Global Search" (styled like a physical magnifying glass).
*   **Left Sidebar (The "Drawer"):** Vertical list of "Notebooks" (Research, Inventory, SEO, Profit). When active, the notebook "opens" on screen.
*   **Main Center (The "Work Surface"):** High-density data tables. Each row is a "Physical Card" that can be "pinned" to the right sidebar.
*   **Right Sidebar (The "Bulletin Board"):** A digital corkboard where you pin "Actionable Notes" (e.g., "Price war on B08X," "Stock out in 5 days").

#### Interaction: The Paperclip Assistant
*   **Visual:** A 3D "Paperclip" character sits on the edge of the screen.
*   **Trigger:** When a "Profit Spike" or "Rank Drop" occurs, the paperclip "holds up" a tiny digital note with the insight.

### Design B: "THE LIVING DASHBOARD"
**Philosophy:** Data feels like a fluid, living ecosystem. Airy, modern, and high-energy.

*   **Theme:** Glassmorphism (Frosted glass + Vibrant gradients).
*   **Background:** Deep navy gradient (#0a0e27 to #1a1a2e).
*   **Primary Panels:** Semi-transparent "Frosted Glass" with a subtle white border.
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   **Active/Primary:** Electric Cyan (#00f0ff).
    *   **Profit/Success:** Neon Lime (#39ff14).
    *   **AI Features:** Soft Purple (#a855f7).
*   **Typography:** 
    *   **Headers:** *Exo 2* (Futuristic, sharp).
    *   **Body:** *Inter* (Clean, modern).

#### Layout Architecture
*   **Center Metric (The "Heartbeat"):** Large, glowing profit number that "breathes" (pulses) with every sale.
*   **Floating Bubbles (KPIs):** Semi-transparent bubbles of different sizes. Hovering reveals a "Liquid Chart."
*   **Action Dock (The "Space Bar"):** Floating glass dock at the bottom for quick navigation.
*   **The "Opportunity Map" Toggle:** Button that "explodes" the table into a 3D Topographical Map of the market.

## 2. NORTH STAR AUTOTHINK: THE AI WORKSPACE

### Design A: "THE SPATIAL CANVAS"
**Philosophy:** Stop thinking in 2D. The AI navigates a 3D space of ideas and code.

*   **Theme:** Spatial Computing (3D Environment + HUD).
*   **Background:** Deep "Space" environment with subtle star particles.
*   **Primary Panels:** Floating "HUD" panels in 3D space.
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   **Primary/AI:** Electric Blue (#00f0ff).
    *   **Nodes/Objects:** Neon Green (#39ff14) and Hot Pink (#ff00ff).
*   **Typography:** *Space Mono* (Futuristic, technical).

#### Layout Architecture
*   **The "Orbital View" (Main Canvas):** Project files are **Nodes floating in a 3D space.** Connected by glowing lines.
*   **The "Navigation Cockpit" (Sidebars):** Settings and Terminal are virtual screens that float in the background.
*   **The "Directional Compass":** A large 3D compass pointing to your "Next Objective."

#### Interaction: The Light Speed Transition
*   **Visual:** When switching sections, the screen "warps" (star-trail effect) into the new view.

---

### Design B: "THE AI ARCHITECT’S STUDIO"
**Philosophy:** The AI is a master architect sketching out a solution for you.

*   **Theme:** Neo-Skeuomorphic (Drafting Tools + Blueprint Paper).
*   **Background:** Dark "Blueprint" grid (Midnight Blue with white grid lines).
*   **Primary Panels:** "Paper" textures and "Wood" toolbars.
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   **Blueprint Blue:** #1e3a5f.
    *   **Ink White:** #f0f0f0.
    *   **Highlighter Yellow:** #ffd700 (AI emphasis).
*   **Typography:** *Architects Daughter* (Handwritten feel) for AI notes; *Roboto Mono* for code.

#### Layout Architecture
*   **The "Main Canvas" (Blueprint Paper):** AI "draws" the project structure here.
*   **The "Drafting Tools" Sidebar:** Icons like pencil (write), ruler (analyze), eraser (delete).
*   **The "Ink Flow" (Code Input):** AI "draws" code in a handwritten font that solidifies into type.
*   **The "Marginalia" (Chat/Thoughts):** AI writes "Notes" in the margins of the blueprint.
