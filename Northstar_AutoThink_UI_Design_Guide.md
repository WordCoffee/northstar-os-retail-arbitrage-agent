# NORTH STAR AUTOTHINK — UI DESIGN GUIDE
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Purpose:** Frontend development reference for the North Star AutothinK website.

---

## 1. DESIGN A: "THE SPATIAL CANVAS"
**Philosophy:** Stop thinking in 2D. The AI navigates a 3D space of ideas and code.

### 1.1 Visual Identity
*   **Theme:** Spatial Computing (3D Environment + HUD).
*   **Background:** A deep "Space" environment with subtle star particles and a dark gradient.
*   **Primary Panels:** Floating "HUD" panels that exist in 3D space, slightly angled toward the user.
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   **Primary/AI:** Electric Blue (#00f0ff).
    *   **Nodes/Objects:** Neon Green (#39ff14) and Hot Pink (#ff00ff).
    *   **Background:** Deep Void Black (#050505).
*   **Typography:** *Space Mono* (Futuristic, technical) for UI; *Fira Code* for code blocks.

### 1.2 Layout Architecture
*   **The "Orbital View" (Main Canvas):** 
    *   Project files and tasks are **Nodes floating in a 3D space.**
    *   You can "zoom in" to see details or "zoom out" to see the whole project structure.
    *   **Connections:** Glowing lines connect related files/tasks, forming a "constellation" of your project.
*   **The "Navigation Cockpit" (Sidebars):** 
    *   Settings and Terminal are virtual screens that float in the 3D background.
    *   They can be "docked" to the side or "freed" to float anywhere.
*   **The "Directional Compass" (Top Left):** 
    *   A large 3D compass in the corner that points toward your "Next Objective" (e.g., "Refactor Module X").

### 1.3 Interaction: The Light Speed Transition
*   **Action:** When you switch between major project sections or "goals."
*   **Visual:** The screen "warps" (a star-trail effect) into the new view, giving a sense of high-speed travel through your codebase.

### 1.4 Components
*   **Nodes:** 3D cubes or spheres that glow when active.
*   **Navigation:** Orbiting rings around the main canvas.
*   **Terminal:** A "floating hologram" at the bottom of the screen.

## 2. DESIGN B: "THE AI ARCHITECT’S STUDIO"
**Philosophy:** The AI is a master architect sketching out a solution for you.

### 2.1 Visual Identity
*   **Theme:** Neo-Skeuomorphic (Drafting Tools + Blueprint Paper).
*   **Background:** A dark "Blueprint" grid (Midnight Blue with white grid lines).
*   **Primary Panels:** "Paper" textures and "Wood" toolbars.
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   **Blueprint Blue:** #1e3a5f (Background).
    *   **Ink White:** #f0f0f0 (Text/Lines).
    *   **Pencil Lead:** #4a4a4a (Secondary text).
    *   **Highlighter Yellow:** #ffd700 (AI emphasis).
*   **Typography:** *Architects Daughter* (Handwritten feel) for AI notes; *Roboto Mono* for code.

### 2.2 Layout Architecture
*   **The "Main Canvas" (Blueprint Paper):** 
    *   A giant white (or dark-grid) Blueprint paper.
    *   The AI "draws" the project structure here.
*   **The "Drafting Tools" Sidebar:** 
    *   A vertical bar of "Drafting Tools" (pencil, ruler, eraser, compass) that represent different AI functions (Write, Measure/Analyze, Delete, Navigate).
*   **The "Ink Flow" (Code Input):** 
    *   When the AI writes code, it doesn't "type"—it "draws" the code in a stylus-like, handwritten-looking font that gradually "solidifies" into professional type.
*   **The "Marginalia" (Chat/Thoughts):** 
    *   Instead of a chat box, the AI writes "Notes to the Architect" (you) in the margins of the blueprint paper.

### 2.3 Interaction: The Eraser
*   **Action:** You can use a "digital eraser" to rub out a section of the AI's plan.
*   **Visual:** The section "smudges" and then the AI "redraws" a new solution in that spot.

### 2.4 Components
*   **Pencils/Tools:** Icons that look like physical wooden pencils or metal rulers.
*   **Notes:** Sticky notes or "taped" pieces of paper attached to the code.
*   **Charts:** Drawn with "ink lines" that look slightly imperfect/hand-drawn.

## 3. UNIVERSAL BRAND ELEMENTS (NORTH STAR)

### 3.1 Logo
*   **Name:** North Star AutothinK.
*   **Icon:** A stylized, glowing 8-pointed star with a hollow center.
*   **Animation:** The star rotates slowly and "pulses" with a soft blue glow.

### 3.2 The "Polaris" Icon System
*   **Style:** Minimalist, 2px stroke, glowing edges when active.
*   **Specific Icons:**
    *   **The Compass Rose:** For "Goal Setting."
    *   **The Branching Tree:** For the "Flow Map."
    *   **The Hourglass-Energy:** For active execution.
    *   **The Mirror:** For the "Diff" view (comparing old vs. new code).
    *   **The Synapse:** A branching neural node icon for the AI core.
    *   **The Shield:** For "Protected Files" (Read-only for the AI).

### 3.3 Color Codes (Hex)
*   **Primary Blue:** #007bff
*   **Success Green:** #00ff9d
*   **Warning Amber:** #D4AF37
*   **Danger Red:** #ff00ff
*   **Background Dark:** #050505
