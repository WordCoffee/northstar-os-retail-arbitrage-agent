Five Agents, One Platform

Each agent specializes in a critical part of the Amazon arbitrage workflow.

🔍

SourceScout

Finds profitable Costco-to-Amazon arbitrage opportunities. Tracks BSR history, seller crowding, price wars, and stockout risk. Scores every product on margin, velocity, and competition.

✍️

ListingForge

AI-generated titles, bullets, descriptions, and backend keywords. Rufus AI readiness scoring. Brand voice enforcement. Compliance checking against Amazon TOS before you publish.

📈

AdPilot

Amazon PPC optimization with four-tier campaign structure. Automated bid management, negative keyword detection, budget allocation, and target ACoS calculation.

📱

SocialPulse

Brand-aware content generation across Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook. Content calendars, hashtag engines, review request emails, and influencer outreach templates.

🧠

AutothinK

Natural language AI workspace. Ask questions, run analysis, orchestrate all agents. Voice interface. Your data stays on your machine — always.

🔒

Private by Design

Local LLM via Ollama. No data sent to cloud APIs. Memory Bank learns your preferences over time. Full audit trail. Your competitive advantage stays yours.
